The First Sales Brochure for The William Vale

In 2016, The William Vale, a 21-story luxury hotel in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, was preparing to open without a marketing team. The sales team needed collateral for road shows and trade events, but had only raw images and architectural descriptions. I planned, wrote and designed the hotel's first sales brochure in two weeks.

  1. The problem

    No branded sales material before opening, while competitors had established collateral.

  2. What I did

    Structured the content with sales leadership, wrote the copy and designed a two-sided brochure.

  3. The result

    800 copies printed in three weeks and adopted by sales, front desk, concierge and events.

01Design requirements

The brochure was an interim tool, so it had to look established while staying simple to update.

  • Visual styleMatch the clean, architectural look of the building.
  • SimplicityKeep the layout simple for a pre-opening, interim piece.
  • UpdatableStructure content so images and details can be swapped easily.
  • Sales orderLocation, rooms, amenities, dining, events.
  • BalanceLifestyle appeal alongside facts: room count, event sizes, access.
  • LegibilityHigh contrast that reads across a trade-show floor.

04Outcomes

800

100%

Of the sales team used it, then Front Desk, Concierge and Events.

Framework

The new marketing team built the official brochure on its structure.

Delivered on time for pre-opening sales and used to support early group and corporate bookings.

05Lessons learned

Step beyond the role

Early-stage teams need people to fill gaps outside their job description.

Stay close to sales

Working directly with stakeholders kept every page tied to selling priorities.

Internal tools scale

A piece built for one team was adopted across departments.

06Next time

  1. Stronger imagery through earlier work with marketing, operations and photography.
  2. Guest input from Front Desk and Concierge on the questions guests ask most.
  3. More formats, such as one-page pitch sheets and detailed booklets for event planners.
