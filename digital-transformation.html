3.3 · Digital Transformation · Panopto

Reframing Video for Digital Transformation

After COVID, SK Telecom's use of Panopto dropped and a cancellation was on the table. My champion at SKT liked the product, but couldn't get budget approved because leadership saw no reason to keep paying. I rebuilt the story: from a pandemic-era video library to a platform for social learning and knowledge sharing.

  1. The problem

    Leadership saw Panopto as a COVID tool with no post-pandemic use case.

  2. What I did

    Analyzed usage, researched the DX market and peers, and designed an executive pitch deck.

  3. The result

    SKT renewed for a year despite a plan to cancel.

06Lessons learned

Story over features

The obstacle was a missing narrative, not the product.

Reframe the decline

Falling usage can be told as lost potential.

Neutral design builds trust

A data-first deck read as advice, not a sales pitch.

Borrow credibility

Analysts and peers made the decision feel less risky.

07Next time

  1. Arm the champion early with a budget-ready story, before renewal season.
  2. Make ROI measurable with a calculator and a live dashboard, not one-off slides.
  3. Grow a champion network inside the account so one contact isn't the only voice.
