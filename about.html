Floor 4 · About me

Different rooms,
one story.

I'm an avid museum-goer. In every gallery, objects that seem to have nothing in common begin to tell one story once a curator puts them in the same room.

My career is one of those rooms: hotel sales in New York, partner growth at Expedia, customer success across Asia-Pacific. Hospitality taught me to read people. Technology taught me to read systems.

Design is where they meet. Like a curator, I bring people, their needs and the systems around them together into one clear, thoughtful experience.

“Let your skills make a living, but let your passions build a life.”
