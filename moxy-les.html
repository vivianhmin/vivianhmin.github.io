Moxy LES: A Hotel Concept for the Lower East Side

A full hotel concept and investor pitch for a Moxy by Marriott at 196 Orchard Street. The project combined neighborhood research, site analysis, a floor-by-floor concept and a five-year financial model to test whether an affordable, design-forward hotel could work in the Lower East Side.

  1. The problem

    The LES had cultural demand but no affordable, branded lifestyle hotel for younger travelers.

  2. What we did

    Researched the market, designed the hotel floor by floor, and modeled cost, revenue and returns.

  3. The result

    An A against a class average of B-. A Moxy later opened in the Lower East Side.

01The gap

The Lower East Side was evolving. Its hotel scene wasn't.

Boutique hotels

Expensive, and exclusive or intimidating to younger travelers.

Chain hotels

Generic design with little cultural relevance in the LES.

The opportunity

A branded, affordable hotel that fits how modern LES visitors travel.

Design needs numbers

Financial forecasts gave the concept its credibility.

Speak the investor's language

Every creative idea had to translate into revenue or cost.
