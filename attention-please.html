Attention, Please!: A Poster Series for Amnesty International

What makes someone stop, think, and carry an idea away from a piece of paper they only walked past? I designed three posters for Amnesty International, each built on one image: the thing that causes harm turns into the one it harms.

  1. The challenge

    Make a static poster support a cause without over-explaining it, and still lead people to act.

  2. What I did

    Narrowed Amnesty's 20+ causes to three, tested concepts with viewers, and rebuilt the series around cause and consequence.

  3. The result

    A consistent three-poster series with a clear reading order and a visible call to action.

    03Watch a poster evolve

    Drag through every version I saved. Each one changed one thing: the crop, the hierarchy, then the color.

    04What I learned

    Consistency is a system

    Posters can differ in color and composition and still belong together through shared logic.

    Hierarchy is a decision

    When everything is emphasized, nothing is. Small shifts in weight guide how people read.

    Clarity comes from removal

    What I left out mattered as much as what I kept.
