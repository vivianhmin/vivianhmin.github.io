1.1 · POOM Identity Design · Mission trip, Mongolia 2023

POOM: An Identity Built on an Embrace

Team POOM (Pray Over Our Mongolia) is a faith-based mission team that traveled to Mongolia in 2023. When the team's designer left unexpectedly, I took over and created the visual identity in 13 days: a logo, prayer cards and team T-shirts.

  1. The challenge

    Past materials were functional but forgettable. The new identity had to be bold, meaningful and approachable for older audiences.

  2. What I did

    Designed the logo, prayer cards and T-shirts, then managed printing and vendors.

  3. The result

    500 prayer cards distributed before the trip, and the logo reused for the 2024 mission.

01One word

품다poom-da · to embrace, to hold close to the heart

The team name comes from this Korean verb. The whole identity grew out of one idea: reduce an embrace to a single mark that anyone can read, in any language.
