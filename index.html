About the Curator

Vivian H. Min is a designer with a decade of experience across tech, e-commerce and hospitality, combining design, data and strategy. From hotel sales to enterprise software and customer success across APAC, her work connects individual moments with the systems behind them.

She is pursuing an M.A. in Design at Carnegie Mellon University, Class of 2027.

Floor 4 · About Me

Vivian H. Min

My career began in hospitality, where I learned the value of human connection and emotional intelligence. From there I moved into e-commerce and then enterprise SaaS, where data and clear communication became everyday tools for serving clients.

Working closely with customers, I saw how every product decision shaped their experience. That pulled me toward user experience and product design, where the goal is to anticipate user needs and business goals rather than react to problems after the fact.

“Let your skills make a living, but let your passions build a life.”
