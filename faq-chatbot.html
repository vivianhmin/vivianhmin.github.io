3.2 · FAQ Chatbot · Expedia Group

FAQ Chatbot in a Local Messaging App

In 2019, Expedia's growth in Korea depended on small lodgings: motels, guesthouses and family-run rentals, often run by owners in their 60s and 70s. They struggled with Expedia's self-service tools and called their account managers for every small question. I led a KakaoTalk chatbot, inside the messaging app Korea already uses every day, that answered those questions automatically.

  1. The problem

    Partners signed up quickly but dropped off when operations got confusing, and routine calls ate up account managers' time.

  2. What I did

    Logged every partner call for four weeks, turned the patterns into a Q&A library, and built the bot in KakaoTalk.

  3. The result

    Routine support calls fell by 70% within a month of launch, freeing the team to sign new partners.

02The problem

Every major hotel in Korea was already on Expedia, so growth had to come from smaller properties. Local competitors onboarded these owners by phone. Expedia asked them to manage everything through a global web portal and email support, which many found hard to use.

01

02

Step 2 · Turn repeats into a question map

Duplicates were merged and grouped into the five menus partners would see, with 12 main questions and 4 follow-ups each. This map became the chatbot's script.

FAQs for Onboarding New Customers
1Getting Started
  • What information do I need to prepare before registering?
  • How long does the approval process take?
  • Can I edit my property details after the listing goes live?
  • Is there a cost to list my property?
  • Can I add different room types later?
  • How do I set weekday vs weekend pricing?
  • Can I change rates after publishing them?
  • Is there a bulk edit option for rate changes?
  • How do I close out certain dates if my rooms are sold out?
  • Can I connect my channel manager or PMS?
  • How often should I update my inventory?
  • What happens if I forget to update availability?
2Reservation management
  • Will I get notifications for new bookings?
  • How do I cancel or modify a booking if needed?
  • What if a guest doesn't show up?
  • How soon do I get paid after a guest's stay?
  • Who pays the refund if the guest cancels?
  • Can I set a cancellation policy for my property?
  • What happens if I want to waive a cancellation fee?
  • Does Expedia notify me if a guest cancels?
3Payments
  • When will I get paid for completed bookings?
  • Can I change my bank account details later?
  • Are there service fees or deductions from payouts?
  • How do I view or download payment reports?
  • How is commission calculated?
  • Can the commission rate change over time?
  • Is there a way to negotiate a lower commission?
  • Are there extra fees I should know about?
  • Does Expedia collect taxes on my behalf?
  • How do I issue tax invoices to guests if needed?

  • What factors affect search ranking on Expedia?

  • How do I get better reviews and ratings?

  • How do Expedia promotions work?
  • Can I create discounts for specific dates or seasons?

5Support
  • Can I call someone if the chatbot can't help?
  • Is there an emergency support line for urgent booking issues?

  • How do I price competitively against other local properties?

  • It became the knowledge base the conversation flows were built on.

06Challenges

No engineering support

The whole solution had to be built with tools the team could run ourselves.

Partners liked calling

We positioned the bot as faster and available around the clock, and adoption followed.

07Lessons learned

Adapt locally

Korean partners needed more guidance than U.S. partners. Meeting them in KakaoTalk made support feel familiar instead of foreign.

Protect growth time

Structured, repeatable answers cut churn risk and gave account managers back the time to grow the business.

08Next time

  1. Measure retention. Track churn over time to quantify long-term impact.
  2. Share earlier. Bring results to other APAC teams sooner for feedback and wider adoption.
  3. Go beyond text. Add step-by-step onboarding guides or short video tutorials.
